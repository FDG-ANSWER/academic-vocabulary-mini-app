Academic Vocabulary Mini-App

Learn key academic vocabulary and test your understanding with a short quiz.

How to use this app

  1. Open the Learn section and read each word, meaning and example sentence.
  2. Open the Quiz section when you are ready to practise.
  3. Select an answer for each question and read the feedback.
  4. Review your final score and retry the quiz if you want more practice.
