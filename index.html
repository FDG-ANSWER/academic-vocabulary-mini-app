Learn Academic Vocabulary

Read each word, its meaning and an example sentence before attempting the quiz.

Analyse

Meaning: To examine something carefully in order to understand it.

Example: Students should analyse the results before writing their conclusion.

Approach

Meaning: A method or way of dealing with a task or problem.

Example: The researcher used a practical approach to collect data.

Assess

Meaning: To evaluate or make a judgement about something.

Example: The tutor will assess the assignment using the marking criteria.

Concept

Meaning: An idea or principle used to understand a subject.

Example: The lecture introduced the concept of sustainable development.

Context

Meaning: The situation or conditions in which something happens.

Example: The quotation must be understood in its historical context.

Data

Meaning: Facts or information collected for study or analysis.

Example: The survey data showed that most students preferred online feedback.

Evaluate

Meaning: To judge the quality, value or effectiveness of something.

Example: The report evaluates the strengths and limitations of the study.

Significant

Meaning: Important or large enough to have an effect.

Example: The findings showed a significant difference between the two groups.
